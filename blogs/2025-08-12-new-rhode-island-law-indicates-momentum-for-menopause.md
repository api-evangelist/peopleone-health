Rhode Island has become the first U.S. state to require workplace accommodations for menopause, under its Fair Employment Practices Act—emphasizing the need for proactive, inclusive healthcare support. This shift underscores the growing importance of accessible, employer-enabled health solutions—precisely what People One Health delivers through its advanced primary care model and preventive care focus.
